# Repair service

## Paid repairs
Items outside warranty can be repaired for a flat fee. Zipper replacement costs 18 dollars. Patching a tent floor costs 25 dollars. Re-waterproofing a jacket costs 30 dollars.

## Turnaround
Repairs take 10 to 14 business days after we receive the item. We email you a quote before any work if the repair is not on the flat-fee list.
