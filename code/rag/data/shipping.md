# Shipping

## Delivery speeds
Standard shipping takes 4 to 6 business days and costs 6 dollars. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs 15 dollars. Orders over 75 dollars ship standard for free.

## Where we ship
We ship to all 50 US states and to Canada. We do not ship to PO boxes with express delivery. Canadian orders may have import duties that the customer pays on delivery.

## Tracking
You get a tracking email within 24 hours of your order leaving our warehouse. Orders placed after 2 pm Eastern leave the next business day.
