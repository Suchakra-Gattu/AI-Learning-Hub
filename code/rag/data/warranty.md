# Warranty

## What is covered
All Northwind backpacks, tents and jackets have a 5 year warranty against defects in materials and stitching. Zippers that fail under normal use are covered.

## What is not covered
Normal wear, UV fading, damage from fire or sharp objects, and damage from using gear in ways it was not made for are not covered.

## How to make a claim
Email warranty@northwind.example with your order number and two photos of the problem. We reply within 3 business days. If the claim is approved we repair the item or send a replacement. We do not pay for the shipping of the damaged item to us for claims older than 1 year.
