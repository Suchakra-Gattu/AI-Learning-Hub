# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Tents and sleeping bags can be returned within 60 days if they have not been slept in.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method. After we receive your return, the refund takes 5 to 7 business days to show up. Shipping costs are not refunded unless we sent the wrong item.

## Items we cannot take back
Fuel canisters, opened water filters, and custom-fit boots cannot be returned for safety and hygiene reasons. Gift cards are not refundable.
